You're right to call that out. Here's the proper flow as I understand it:

Before writing any job — check who's online

The RTDB online/620611/ node already has live driver presence. Before accepting the booking, the app checks:

Nobody online at all → don't even write a job. Tell the passenger immediately: "Invercargill Taxis is offline right now — no drivers available." Done. No waiting.
If there ARE drivers online, write the job, then branch:

Driver is free (no current trip)
→ Dispatcher offers the job directly to them
→ Driver's app flashes the offer
→ Driver accepts within ~30 seconds
→ Passenger sees driver name, vehicle, ETA straight away

Driver is online but busy (currently on a trip)
→ Job sits in the dispatcher as unassigned / queued
→ Passenger sees: "You're in the queue — the driver is finishing a current trip. Estimated wait: ~X mins"
→ When driver completes that trip, the offer flashes on their screen
→ Driver accepts → passenger gets notified and ride proceeds

Driver doesn't accept in time (offer expires)
→ Dispatcher re-queues or tries again
→ If nobody accepts after exhausting all options → "Unable to find a driver right now, please try again"

The 2-minute silent cancel is wrong because:

It doesn't tell the passenger why (busy vs offline vs no response)
A queued job that was never accepted shouldn't vanish after 2 minutes — it should stay live until the driver is free
The passenger should see real queue status, not just "searching..." forever
